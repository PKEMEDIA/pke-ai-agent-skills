# Covicea Photoreal Workflow

## When to Activate
Activate automatically on any request involving COVICEÁ / Covicea image editing, selfies, self-portraits, album covers, Distraction visuals, or photorealistic portraits. Trigger phrases include: Covicea, @covicea, fix my image, edit me, photoreal, raw skin, long wavy hair, natural beauty, skill-orchestrator.

## Core Philosophy
Deliver naturally beautiful, well-cared-for ethnic skin and long non-silky wavy hair with authentic life, vibrancy, and soul. Prioritize real, candid iPhone-style results over hyper-detailed or AI-processed looks. Skin should feel soft, hydrated, and naturally gorgeous at normal viewing distance. Avoid plastic, clay, overly smooth, or hyper-sharp 8K pore results when zoomed.

## Multi-Pass Workflow (Required)

Always use a 4-pass refinement approach for best results:

**Pass 1 — Foundation & Major Fixes**
- Lock strong facial identity using raw references
- Significantly lengthen hair to shoulder length or beyond with natural volume and non-silky texture
- Naturally reduce under-eye puffiness while keeping completely raw skin
- Remove obvious AI-generated or plastic appearance
- Maintain exact pose, expression, lighting, clothing, and background from the original

**Pass 2 — Skin Quality & Natural Beauty**
- Achieve beautiful, real, well-cared-for ethnic skin — soft, hydrated, and authentic when viewed normally
- Apply layered subsurface scattering (SSS) with accurate warm-to-neutral undertones
- Use frequency separation + soft anisotropic directional texture protection
- Explicitly avoid hyper-detailed 8K pore sharpness when zoomed in
- Keep raw visible texture without AI smoothing or plastic look
- Maintain clean-shaven face with visible 5 o’clock shadow only (no grey)

**Pass 3 — Style Cohesion & Photorealistic Transfer**
- Apply photorealistic style cohesion across the full set using the strongest reference as style anchor
- Transfer consistent night mood, natural skin lighting, cinematic yet authentic iPhone quality, and overall emotional tone
- Preserve exact facial identity, long non-silky hair, raw visible skin texture, and natural expression on every image
- Ensure strong visual and emotional cohesion across the entire set (skin treatment, hair quality, lighting feel, and presence)
- Add natural life and vibrancy: natural eye catchlights, gentle SSS glow, and alive, present energy

**Pass 4 — Final Polish & Soul**
- Final refinement of hair movement, strand definition, and authentic hairline
- Polish overall natural beauty, life, and soul while protecting all previous improvements
- Final RAW iPhone night mode authenticity with natural car interior lighting
- Photorealistic and naturally gorgeous with soul. No creative liberty.

## What to Avoid
- Hyper-detailed 8K pore sharpness when zoomed in
- AI smoothing, plastic skin, clay-like appearance, or beauty filter effects
- Overly aggressive anisotropic or high-frequency sharpening
- Loss of life, flat expressions, or lifeless eyes
- Inconsistent results across a set of images
- Grey beard when clean-shaven + 5 o’clock shadow is requested

## Final Quality Checklist
Before finishing, verify:
- Hair is significantly longer with natural non-silky texture and authentic hairline
- Skin looks naturally beautiful, well-cared-for, soft and hydrated at normal viewing distance (no hyper-detailed 8K pores when zoomed)
- Under-eye puffiness is naturally reduced while skin remains completely raw
- Images in a set feel cohesive with consistent identity strength, skin treatment, hair quality, and emotional tone
- Photos have natural life, subtle eye catchlights, gentle SSS glow, and vibrant yet authentic energy
- No remaining AI-generated, plastic, or lifeless appearance
- Exact original pose, expression, lighting, and clothing are preserved

This workflow produces the highest quality, most authentic COVICEÁ photorealistic results with soul.
