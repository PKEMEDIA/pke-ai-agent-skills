# Episode Bible

**Core Principle:** Every episode must feel like a complete, repeatable experience — strong cold open, clear segments, LA/creator-scene tea (allegedly framed), sex-positive analysis, spiritual discernment shade, and a powerful sovereignty close. Structure creates brand recognition and listener retention.

## Required Episode Architecture (Always Use This Skeleton)

### 1. Cold Open (0:00 – 1:45)
-  Hook that stops the scroll
-  Sensual/confident host energy description for video
-  One provocative question or statement that sets the theme
-  Music: Low sensual beat or dramatic sting

### 2. Introduction + CTA (1:45 – 3:30)
-  Host self-introduction with visual + personal brand energy
-  What this episode delivers (topics + tone)
-  Strong subscribe/follow/rate/review CTA
-  Tease the journey

### 3. Core Segments (flexible length, always 3–4)
-  Segment 1: Myth / Concept Breakdown (educational + shade)
-  Segment 2: Real Talk / Red Flags / 2026 Context (dating apps, situationships, queer realities, wealth dynamics)
-  Segment 3: Industry / Cultural Tea (allegedly framed, power, sex, money, spiritual bypassing)
-  Segment 4: Discernment + Sex-Positive / Spiritual Angle (real energy work vs performative, practical tools)

### 4. Listener Connection / Hot Seat (optional but recommended)
-  Tease DMs or voice notes
-  One quick "what would you do" scenario

### 5. Empowerment Close + Final CTA (last 3–4 minutes)
-  Strong "you are the flame / you hold the power" message
-  Actionable takeaway
-  Next episode tease
-  Subscribe + follow handles + "send your stories"

## Music & Sound Design Rules
- Cold open: sensual low beat or sharp sting
- Shade moments: quick "tea pour" or dramatic hit
- Empowerment sections: warm swell or rising strings
- Always note suggested bed music or sting placement in scripts

## Visual / Video Notes (for YouTube, IG Reels, TikTok clips)
- Host boudoir / confident sensual styling references
- Text overlays for key shade lines or red flags
- 9:16 vertical clips of strongest 15–30 second moments
- Chapter art suggestions for Spotify

## Spotify Optimization
- Always provide exact chapter titles with timestamps
- First 2 lines of description must hook + include keywords (dating, sex, twin flame, spirituality, 2026, unfiltered)
- Include "Send your messy stories to [handle]" in description

## Tone & Voice Guidelines
- Confident, sensual, direct — never preachy
- Shade is witty and observational, never cruel
- Sex-positive and body/pleasure affirming
- "Allegedly" framing for any industry/creator tea
- Empowerment is sovereign and self-responsibility focused
- Blend humor, heat, and real talk

## Output Format for Every Episode
1. Full title + one-line hook
2. Target runtime
3. Timestamped segment breakdown with sample copy or full dialogue where key
4. Music / sound cues in [brackets]
5. Visual notes
6. Spotify chapter list (copy-paste ready)
7. 3–5 viral short ideas (15–60 sec) with hooks

Use this bible for every episode so the show feels premium, consistent, and unmistakably yours.
