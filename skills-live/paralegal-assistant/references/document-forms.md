# Document forms

Use the forum's own form when one exists. These are structural checklists, not substitutes.

## Affidavit

Caption/venue · affiant identity · numbered factual paragraphs (one fact each) · how the affiant knows · exhibits incorporated · sworn verification under penalty of perjury · signature · notary.

## Complaint / petition / statement of claim

Caption · jurisdiction and venue · parties · factual allegations supporting each count · claims with elements · prayer for relief · verification · jury demand if applicable.

## Contract

Parties · recitals · definitions · operative provisions · representations · covenants · conditions · remedies · termination · governing law/venue · signatures.

## Demand / preservation letter

Professional tone · short factual summary · legal basis · specific demand · reasonable deadline · stated consequence of silence · how to comply · professional close.

## Exhibit cover

Exhibit letter/number · date of document · one-line description · Bates or page range · source (who produced it).
