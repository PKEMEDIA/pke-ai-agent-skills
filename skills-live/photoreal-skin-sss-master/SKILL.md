# Photoreal Skin Sss Master

## Overview

This skill provides expert techniques for rendering hyper-realistic skin using advanced subsurface scattering (SSS) models and lighting interactions. It is optimized for medium-deep to deep brown skin (Covicea Bible Skin standard) with phenotype-accurate warm internal glow, soft volumetric quality, organic multi-scale texture, natural irregular pore variation, and seamless integration with textured non-silky hair. Fully supports authentic iPhone 15 Pro Photonic Engine / TrueDepth computational look.

## Instructions

Apply these techniques for skin in all photoreal portrait and iPhone selfie work:

**Subsurface Scattering & Physics (Dipole / Advanced)**
- Implement dipole or advanced multi-pole SSS models (inspired by Jensen dipole and Donner multipole) to produce accurate warm reddish or golden subsurface glow in ears, nose, lips, and shadow transitions while maintaining overall skin tone.
- Use anatomical anchors for SSS: emphasize glow in earlobes, nasal ala, nostrils, fingertips, and thinner tissue areas where light penetrates and scatters.
- For Covicea / medium-deep to deep brown skin (Bible Skin standard): target rich medium-deep to deep brown with accurate warm-to-neutral undertones, phenotype-accurate subsurface scattering with warm internal glow and soft volumetric quality. Completely natural makeup-free skin that looks healthy, well-hydrated and moisturized. Higher melanin shortens mean free path (more compact diffusion). Spectral behavior: shorter wavelengths scatter more superficially, longer penetrate deeper for natural warm color bleeding.
- Render multi-scale photographic texture with visible natural pores, micro-occlusion, subtle oil sheen, and healthy luminosity — never over-smooth or plastic. Apply HSL protection to control orange/red channels.
- Prompt language examples that work well: "skin shows natural directional subsurface scattering with light penetrating and diffusing asymmetrically", "multi-layer subsurface scattering simulating epidermal, dermal, and hypodermal depth", "spectrally accurate subsurface scattering with warm bloom beneath highlights".

**Lighting Interaction & iPhone Realism**
- Control lighting so directional or ambient iPhone-style light creates realistic subsurface scattering that responds correctly to skin thickness, blood flow, and undertones.
- In iPhone selfies: skin should show natural micro-texture and slight sensor grain matching real iPhone photos. Avoid perfect studio skin.
- Soft natural window or ambient lighting produces the most believable SSS and catchlights.

**Hair-Skin Integration (Critical for Textured Hair)**
- Ensure seamless integration at hairlines and edges: hair strands interact with skin SSS glow, cast soft realistic shadows, and flyaways receive subtle illumination from skin.
- Hairline blending must feel organic — subtle root variation and light scattering at the transition zone.

**Mulatto / Mixed Skin Specifics**
- Clearly specify undertones in prompts: warm golden olive, honey, or caramel on medium brown base.
- Allow natural variation in tone across face (cheeks slightly different from forehead, etc.).
- SSS should reveal subtle natural flush and blood flow appropriate to the undertone.

**Negative Prompts**
Use the full categorized negative prompt sets from the negative-prompt-library skill, especially the Core + Skin block + Mulatto Skin additions. This provides the strongest defense against plastic/waxy skin and incorrect undertones.

**Integration with Other Skills**
- Use alongside photoreal-undetectable-portrait-master as the primary workflow.
- Pair with photoreal-hair-anisotropic-master for complete hair-skin physics interaction.
- For Covicea subjects: maintain rich skin with warm undertones and natural iPhone texture.

**iPhone Selfie Specific**
- Skin in iPhone photos has characteristic slight grain, natural dynamic range, and micro texture. Include these in prompts/descriptions for authenticity.
- Avoid over-polished or commercial beauty lighting — favor casual natural light.
