# 2257 & AI Content — Short Ops Notes (PKE)

**Not legal advice.** Studio ops summary for Pretty Kitty Entertainment / Coviceá. When in doubt, ask counsel.

---

## AI-generated is not a 2257 substitute

- **18 U.S.C. § 2257 / 2257A** record-keeping applies to **real human performers** in sexually explicit productions, not to purely synthetic images/video with no real person depicted.  
- Generating AI stills/video **does not** create 2257 records — and **does not** replace records when real talent appears.  
- Do **not** treat an AI “character card” or LoRA folder as age documentation for a real person.

---

## Mixing real talent + AI

| Scenario | Action |
|----------|--------|
| 100% AI, no real person depicted | No 2257 performer file for the synthetic cast; still keep internal **manifest** (models, date, AI label) |
| Real performer + AI bodies/faces in same post | Maintain **full 2257 records for every real performer**; document which assets are AI in the shoot manifest |
| Real performer face on AI body (or reverse) | High risk / platform-policy sensitive — prefer not to; if ever done under counsel guidance, records for the real person still required |
| Real product in AI scene | Product isn’t 2257; performer rules unchanged |

Keep custodian path and ID copies for real talent exactly as your existing live-shoot SOP requires.

---

## Labeling AI content

- Where **OnlyFans, X, and other platforms require** AI/synthetic labeling or disclosure, **label it**.  
- Internal filenames / `manifest.md`: mark `AI-generated: yes/no/mixed`.  
- Captions: follow Brand + platform rules; don’t imply a real named person appears if they don’t.  
- Do not use AI to impersonate a real performer who didn’t consent.

---

## No minors ever

- **Zero** content — AI or real — depicting minors, teen coding, “barely legal,” school settings, or youthful face prompts.  
- Prompt bans and negatives are **controls**, not a defense if someone ships prohibited material.  
- If a gen reads underage: **delete**, don’t “fix with inpaint.”

---

## Locked identities

- **Face Lock** and **Black Mask** remain **LOCKED**. Do not train, unlock, or publish those faces. Unrelated to 2257 but binding studio policy.

---

## Quick do / don’t

**Do:** age-verify real performers; keep 2257 for real talent; label AI when required; delete underage-looking gens.  
**Don’t:** claim AI exempts mixed shoots from records; use AI to fake a real person’s participation; ship unlabeled AI where the platform mandates disclosure.
