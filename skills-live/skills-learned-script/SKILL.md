# Skills Learned Script

## Core Purpose
Transform raw lists of skills, experiences, and learnings into polished, compelling, professional narratives and scripts. Produce resume-ready sections, interview answers, cover letter paragraphs, LinkedIn summaries, or spoken scripts that highlight growth, impact, and unique value.

## Step-by-Step Process
1. **Gather and Categorize**: Collect all skills, experiences, and learnings. Group into clear categories such as Business & Operations, Creative Production, Technical & Tools, Leadership & Soft Skills, Industry-Specific Knowledge. Identify cross-cutting themes like problem-solving, adaptability, or client focus.

2. **Prioritize and Select**: Choose the most relevant and impressive items for the target audience or role. Focus on recent, impactful, or unique skills. Limit to 4-7 per category for scannability.

3. **Apply Strong Structure**:
   - Start with a powerful opening statement or hook that frames your expertise.
   - Use action-oriented language and quantifiable achievements where possible (e.g., "Managed $500K budgets delivering 20% growth" rather than "Managed budgets").
   - Weave in context: what was learned, how it was applied, and the result or transformation.
   - End with forward-looking statement on how these skills will add value.

4. **Polish for Impact**:
   - Ensure concise yet vivid language. Eliminate jargon unless audience-specific.
   - Balance technical depth with accessibility.
   - Incorporate storytelling elements for engagement (challenge → action → result).
   - Align tone with brand or personal voice (professional, creative, empowered, etc.).
   - Optimize for readability: short paragraphs, bullet points for resumes, flowing prose for scripts.

5. **Tailor and Adapt**: Customize for specific contexts — resume bullets for ATS, narrative paragraphs for cover letters, conversational style for interviews, visual-friendly for presentations. Prepare variations for different lengths or emphases.

6. **Review and Refine**: Check for consistency, accuracy, and authenticity. Ensure it reflects real capabilities without exaggeration. Test by reading aloud for scripts. Gather feedback if possible and iterate.

## Output Formats
- **Resume Section**: Categorized bullets with impact statements.
- **Interview Script**: 60-90 second answers to "Tell me about your skills" or "What have you learned?" with STAR method examples.
- **Cover Letter Paragraph**: 3-5 sentences weaving skills into narrative.
- **LinkedIn/About Summary**: First-person professional bio highlighting journey and expertise.
- **Presentation Script**: Talking points with hooks, stories, and calls to action.
- **Portfolio or Bio Page**: Engaging prose with sections.

## Quality Standards
- Authentic and confident tone without arrogance.
- Specific and evidence-based rather than generic.
- Forward-looking and value-oriented.
- Culturally sensitive and inclusive language.
- Optimized for both human readers and ATS/algorithms where relevant.

## Edge Cases & Nuances
- For career changers or non-linear paths: Emphasize transferable skills and the learning journey as strength.
- For creative or non-traditional roles: Blend hard skills with creative process and unique perspective.
- When skills are emerging or self-taught: Highlight initiative, rapid learning, and application examples.
- For senior or leadership roles: Focus on strategic impact, mentorship, and vision enabled by skills.
- Avoid over-claiming or listing every minor tool; prioritize depth and relevance.
